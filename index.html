About this Azimut 72 Fly

Seller highlightCaptain maintained, one owner from new, full Azimut service history at the Miami yard. Never chartered. Hydraulic swim platform and Seakeeper 9 fitted at build.

This 2022 Azimut 72 Fly is a third generation hull with the four cabin, four head layout: a full beam owner's suite amidships, a VIP forward and two twin guest cabins, plus a two berth crew cabin aft. Twin MAN V12 1400 engines give a 27 knot cruise and 32 knots top, with 410 hours logged since delivery in March 2022.

The flybridge carries the second helm, a wet bar with grill, and a hardtop with an opening section over the sunpads. The cockpit connects to the saloon through a full height glass door, and the galley sits aft in the saloon for serving both areas. Electronics are Raymarine Axiom Pro with two 16 inch displays at each helm, FLIR night camera and Seakeeper 9 gyro stabiliser.

Lying Miami, FL, available for viewing now. US duty paid. Price reduced 14 August 2026 from $2,250,000.
